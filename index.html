      List format details
      • Separators: - , – , =, : or a tab. E-Mail stays one word.
      • A line with just A1…B2 (or Level B1, # B2) sets the category for the lines below it.
      • (B1) or [B1] at the end of a line sets that word's category.
      • German example sentence after | : dog - der Hund | Der Hund bellt.
