List format
  • One word per line, German first: der Hund - dog
  • Separators: - , – , =, : or a tab
  • A line with just A1…C2 (or Level B1, # B2) sets the level for the words below it
  • (B1) or [B1] at the end of a line overrides the level
  • Your own categories work too: # Travel on its own line, or (Travel) at the end
  • Optional example sentence after | : der Hund - dog | Der Hund bellt.
Voice & speed
Speed

If words sound English, install a German voice: Android: Settings → Text-to-speech → Speech services by Google → Install voice data → Deutsch. iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → German. Then reload the app.

Categories & backup

    Tip: tap a word's coloured badge to move it to another category. In a pasted list, a line like # Travel starts a new category.
